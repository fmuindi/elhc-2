Who We Are

Our Vision & Beliefs

We are Firebrand Nation — people on fire for Christ.

Our Vision

ELHC is a ministry taking back enemy-held territory — making an impact from the inside out by reaching the hurting, broken, and hopeless.

We are dedicated to seeing people restored, made whole, and empowered to go back and impact the lives of others. Committed to taking the gospel to the world, rebuilding communities, and empowering people to become spiritually, physically, and financially stable — establishing God's covenant in the Earth.

What We Believe

ELHC preaches the full gospel of the Word of God, allowing the Holy Spirit to lead our services. We expect signs, wonders, and miracles.

We believe in allowing the Holy Spirit to lead and guide our services according to Mark 16:20. A world that has come up against a wall of fear needs a church on fire for the Lord. We believe God calls ordinary people and transforms them into extraordinary firebrands for His use.

Our Legacy

We Know Who We Are

"We know who we are because we know where we have come from."

Dr. James H. Davis grew up in Mechanicsville, Knoxville, Tennessee. Born in 1956, he lived most of his childhood during the height of the civil rights movement, raised by his mother and grandmother with a foundation of hope and resilience.

As a young man he began drifting toward street life — until one night God broke through. After that prayer, he spent the next 25 years on a mission, using God as his weapon to evangelize, organize, and truly save lives.

Honoring Our Founder — Dr. James H. Davis

Today more than ever our efforts to take the gospel to the world are stronger than ever before. We honor our founder by living out his legacy daily. Together we are Firebrand Nation.
